Privacy Policy for School Time Table

Last updated: July 2026

The School Time Table mobile application does not collect, transmit, or store any personal or sensitive user data.

Data Storage

All timetable records, periods, and schedules you enter are stored locally on your own device using a local database system (Hive). No data is sent to external servers or shared with third parties.

Permissions

The app does not require any runtime permissions, internet access, camera, location, or contacts access.

Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about this Privacy Policy, feel free to contact the developer.

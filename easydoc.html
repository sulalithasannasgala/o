Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This privacy policy applies to the D (EasyDoc) mobile application (Package: com.woodbulldozer.easydoc). This app is built as a Free app and is intended for use as is.

1. Information Collection and Use

This application does not collect, store, or share any personal user data. All document generation (PDF creation, printing, and file sharing) happens locally on your device.

2. Storage Permissions

On Android 11 and above, this application uses scoped temporary storage to save PDFs before printing or sharing. It does not access your personal device storage without necessity.

3. Third-Party Services

This app does not use any third-party analytics or tracking software. When you share documents via WhatsApp or other apps, their respective privacy policies apply.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions, feel free to contact the developer.
